# Code of Conduct

YKS Defterim aims to maintain a constructive, technical, and welcoming open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- Be respectful and professional
- Focus criticism on code, design, evidence, and outcomes
- Accept review and correction in good faith
- Give clear technical reasons for requested changes
- Respect privacy and do not expose personal information
- Avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces managed by YKS Defterim.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

If you need to report sensitive behavior, avoid publishing private details in a public issue. Contact the maintainer through GitHub and request a private communication channel.
